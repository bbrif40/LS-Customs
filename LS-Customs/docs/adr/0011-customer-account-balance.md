# Show a consolidated customer account balance

Accepted during the design interview on 2026-10-20; implemented locally; target deployment and device/provider checks remain in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md. Provide one customer account-balance page listing individual charges, payments, waivers, and remaining balances, with every entry linked to its booking. Distinguish pending damage proposals from finalized payable charges and identify which outstanding balances block new rentals. This keeps the outstanding account balance separate from a booking's original payment status and makes restrictions explainable when several debts exist.

The page must reflect the agreed settlement and audit history rather than hiding adjustments or treating a pending proposal as an amount already owed. Customers may select which outstanding charge to pay or choose Pay all. Show exactly which balances the payment will settle and record its allocation to those charges. Any remaining blocking balance keeps the rental restriction active. Pay all includes accumulated daily penalties due when quoted. The quote remains valid for 10 minutes: payment confirmed within that window settles the quoted debt in full, without adding a daily penalty that becomes due during the window. If the quote expires, refresh the total before another attempt. This price protection applies to the quoted debt only, not unrelated new charges. If payment succeeds after quote expiry, record and allocate the money received against the quoted debt; do not discard or refund it solely because the quote expired. Show any remaining balance, including applicable penalties after the protected window, and retain rental restrictions until all blocking balances are settled. This debt-payment rule is distinct from the full refund required for a rental payment received after its reservation expired.
