# Secure mechanic capacity before confirming scheduled appointments

Scheduled mechanic appointments must have secured mechanic capacity before being presented as confirmed. Online checkout also requires successful payment; choosing cash instead confirms the appointment immediately with payment due after service. Offer another time before taking payment if capacity is unavailable. This replaces the current payment-before-assignment flow, which can leave a paying customer without an available mechanic; the trade-off is temporarily holding capacity for checkouts that may be abandoned and committing capacity to unpaid cash appointments.

Accepted and refined during the design interview on 2026-10-20. Local implementation is recorded in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md; release checks remain separate. Hold capacity for 10 minutes during checkout with a visible countdown; release it if payment has not succeeded at expiry and check availability again before retrying. Selecting cash converts the checkout hold into a confirmed appointment rather than waiting for payment. Late online payment after expiry enters full-refund processing without restoring the booking (later interview decision). Roadside assistance and vehicle rental cash policies are outside this decision's scope.

Show cash appointments as “Confirmed · Cash due after service.” The mechanic records cash collection after service; admin corrections must be tracked in the audit log. Confirmation and payment collection are separate facts. A mechanic can mark finished work completed even when cash has not been collected; show “Completed · Cash unpaid” and flag it for admin follow-up. Outstanding payment must not misrepresent finished work as still in progress.
