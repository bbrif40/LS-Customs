# Price rental days as 24 hours and charge for late returns

Customers with outstanding late-return charges cannot make new vehicle rental bookings until the charges are settled. Mechanic services and roadside assistance remain available under this restriction. Show a warning alongside the outstanding balance: “Please settle your unpaid late-return charges to book another rental. Your account may be flagged if these charges remain unpaid.” The warning does not itself flag the account; flagging criteria and consequences remain unresolved.

Accepted during the design interview on 2026-10-20; implemented locally; target deployment and device/provider checks remain in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md. Collect both pickup and return times and calculate the agreed rental price using 24-hour rental days measured from pickup, rather than calendar dates alone. A Friday 2 PM pickup with Saturday 2 PM return is one rental day.

After the agreed return time, add a late-return charge to the customer's account at ₱250 for each completed hour late: 59 minutes costs ₱0, 1 hour costs ₱250, 1 hour 59 minutes costs ₱250, and 2 hours costs ₱500. This creates an outstanding obligation separate from the original booking payment. An admin confirms the actual vehicle return time to stop the late-fee clock; default the entry to the current time. Corrections require a reason and an audit trail, and the late-return amount is calculated using the confirmed actual return time. Customers may settle late-return charges through online payment or cash collected and recorded by an admin. Show the amount and its calculation in the customer booking and account, and clear the outstanding balance only after payment is confirmed. Adjustment policies remain unresolved. Recording an amount owed does not authorize an automatic charge to a stored payment method.
